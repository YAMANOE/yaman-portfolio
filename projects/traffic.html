Problem

The road-network import exited zero on every run while failing every insert. Nothing in the logs, nothing in the exit code, nothing in CI. The job reported success and wrote nothing.

My role

BuiltBackend & data layer

ContextPublic-sector pilot · team of 5 · largest contributor

The context line above is the honest split: where it says a team size, the system was a team effort and only the named subsystem was mine.

Approach

The constraint

The routing graph is the foundation of every downstream query. An import that is silently empty is worse than one that fails outright, because the one that fails stops the pipeline and the one that succeeds does not.

What was rejected

Fast imports against provable ones. A verification pass on every import costs time on a job that already runs long, and it buys nothing on the runs that were correct anyway.

What I built

I chose provable: a ratio-based invariant gate that compares what entered against what actually landed, and refuses to proceed when the ratio breaks. It produced a verified 135,000-edge routing graph, and then caught two silent-truncation bugs in the replacement importer I had just written — which is the whole argument for building it.
